Security and reliability

0/14

  • Every query scoped from Auth::clinicId(); cross-tenant patient and queue verified refused.

  • Central matrix; verified per role over HTTP.

  • 128-bit tracking tokens, expiring, no PII, CSRF on the cancel action.

  • New: hashed single-use tokens, 60-minute expiry, enumeration-proof, evicts old sessions.

  • /audit filterable; receptionist forbidden.

  • tools/backup.php plus a restore drill that restores into a scratch DB and checks integrity.

  • docs/CONTINUITY.md describes a paper fallback. Never rehearsed — written, not proven.

  • /api/health with a token: worker backlog, stale sessions, long waits, backup age, schema parity.

  • docs/OPERATIONS.md is an on-call runbook. No paging integration and no incident timeline tool.

  • New: closure starts a retention clock; tools/purge.php is CLI-only, refuses early, keeps invoices, and preserves the clinic row.

  • 145 automated checks plus adversarial curl probes each session. No external penetration test, no dependency CVE scan.
